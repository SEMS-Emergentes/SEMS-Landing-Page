The problem

Your bill arrives already decided

A commercial tariff charges energy by time band and adds a power charge set by the single highest peak of the month. The bill shows the result, never the cause.

⏱

You find out thirty days late

The peak that sets your power charge may last fifteen minutes. You learn about it when the bill arrives and the surcharge is already applied.

🏬

You cannot tell where it came from

The bill is aggregated per supply point. It does not say whether it was refrigeration, the air conditioning or the ovens, so you do not know where to act.

🏢

You cannot compare your sites

With several branches there is no simple way to tell whether one of them is spending more than the rest for its size and format.

How it works

From the switchboard to the saving, in four steps

Measure, understand and act. No electrical background required.

  1. 1

    Register your organization and sites

    Create the chain, add each site with its tariff category and contracted power, and split it into zones.

  2. 2

    Connect the meters

    Each meter is assigned to a site and, if you want, to a zone. From that moment its readings feed the platform.

  3. 3

    Watch demand in real time

    SEMS compares the measured demand against your contracted power and warns you while there is still headroom left.

  4. 4

    Act before the month closes

    Shift load out of peak hours, find the zone that is spending and forecast the bill before it is issued.

Features

Built around the commercial tariff

Not a generic meter: SEMS models peak hours, maximum demand and contracted power the way OSINERGMIN defines them.

📈

Live demand monitoring

Consumption per site and per zone, updated as it happens and expressed in soles, not at the end of the month.

🔔

Demand warning with headroom

A warning while you are still below the contracted power, and a critical alert if you go over it. Avoiding one peak pays for the plan.

🕕

Peak-hour reports

How much of your energy falls between 18:00 and 23:00, and what you would save by moving part of it out of that band.

🗺

Breakdown by zone

Refrigeration, HVAC, lighting, kitchen or warehouse. See which part of the site is behind the consumption.

🧮

Bill forecast

Energy, power charge, excess power and VAT, calculated with the tariff category of each site before the month closes.

⚖️

Benchmarking across sites

Compare branches of a similar size and format, and tell a real deviation from a difference in scale.
